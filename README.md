**Tausi POS by NexaGen Solutions. Simply Outstanding.**

Tausi POS is a touch point-of-sale system for bars and restaurants. Each business runs its own install
on its own network, with no internet needed during service:

- **Tausi Server** (Windows or Linux) holds the data, the business rules and the printing.
- **Tausi Terminal** is the full-screen app on each counter PC (Windows 10/11): PIN login, the live
  table map, ordering, one-button printing of the bill and dispense slips, and settling by the cashier.
- The **admin site** (the same web app in a browser on the network) covers the menu and prices, stock,
  deliveries and counts, the cash book, staff and PINs, permissions, terminals and printers, reports
  and settings.

Every action is checked on the server against a role permission grid, audited, and posted to a
balanced ledger.
