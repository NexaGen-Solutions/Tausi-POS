# User guides

Short guides to print and keep by the counter and in the office, each one or two A4 pages:

| Guide | For | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Waiter](waiter.md) | Log in, tables, ordering with Cold/Warm, Print, voids and requests, My sales | [PDF](pdf/waiter.pdf) |
| [Cashier](cashier.md) | Shifts, settling (cash, M-Pesa, card, split), reprints, pay-outs and drops, the cash-up | [PDF](pdf/cashier.pdf) |
| [Supervisor](supervisor.md) | Approvals, voids and gifts, signing off cash-ups, closing and opening the day | [PDF](pdf/supervisor.pdf) |
| [Owner and admin](owner.md) | Menu, staff, permissions, terminals and printers, stock, cash book, reports, backups, licence, updates, training | [PDF](pdf/owner.pdf) |

Replace `[BUSINESS NAME]` and `[SERVER IP]` for each site before printing.

The pictures come from the demo business, taken by `tools/screenshots/capture.mjs guides`, and the PDFs
are made by `tools/screenshots/guides-pdf.mjs` (docs/RUNNING.md, "Screenshots"). CI makes both on every
push (the `screenshots` artifact).
