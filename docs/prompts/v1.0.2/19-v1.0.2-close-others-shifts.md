**The problem:** when a cashier opens a shift and logs out without closing it, nobody else can close it. Because the day can't close while a shift is open (Task 9), **the whole day gets stuck.**

**The owner's decision:** Administrators and Supervisors can close **any** open shift, and then close the day. This is controlled by a permission, so each business can choose who may do it.

---

## Part A — Permission

**New key:** `cash.close_others_shift`, "Close another cashier's shift", in the Money section of the Permissions grid. Its help text: "Count and close a shift that its cashier left open."

| Permission | Waiter | Cashier | Supervisor | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cash.close_others_shift` | Not allowed | Not allowed | **Allowed** | **Allowed** |

- It can only be **Allowed or Not allowed**. It can't be approved, because the person closing is the approver.
- Set these defaults with a migration, only where the key isn't set yet. Existing installs get the defaults too, since the key is new.
- Closing **one's own** shift works exactly as before (Task 9).
- **Closing the day** still needs `day.open_close`, which Supervisor and Admin already have by default. Confirm this in the report.

## Part B — Where to close another shift

1. **The Close day screen** (Task 9): each open shift in the "blocking" list gets a **Close this shift** button for users with `cash.close_others_shift`. For others, the shift is listed with "Ask a supervisor to close it".
2. **My shift** (Task 9): users with the permission see **Open shifts (n)**, a list of every open shift (cashier, terminal, opened at, cash taken so far), each with **Close this shift**.
3. **The admin site:** **Cash book → Open shifts**, with the same list and button, so it can be done from the office too.

**Any terminal** can be used. The shift doesn't have to be closed on the terminal it was opened on, since the cashier may be gone and the drawer may need to be counted elsewhere.

## Part C — Closing someone else's shift

The same steps as a normal close (Task 9), with these differences:

1. **Header:** "Closing CAROL's shift · Counter A · opened Sun 21:04". Above it, an orange note: "You're closing another cashier's shift. The cash must be counted now."
2. **Reason (required):**
    - Cashier left without closing
    - Cashier is ill or absent
    - End of day
    - Other, with a note
3. **The count:** the person closing **counts the drawer** by denomination, as in Task 9.
    - **Blind count:** if `cashup.blind` is on, the expected figure stays hidden until the count is submitted, as for cashiers.
4. **Sign-off:**
    - If **another** logged-in user has `day.open_close`, they sign off with their PIN, as usual.
    - If **no one else** is available (the supervisor is alone), the closer may sign off **themselves**, but must tick "I counted this cash alone".
    - A shift signed off by the person who counted it is **flagged** "Closed and signed off by the same person" on the slip, the Payments and cash-ups report, and the audit log.
5. **Everything else** is as in Task 9:
    - the difference is posted to Cash over/short
    - the drawer is moved to the safe
    - the shift is locked
6. **Cash-up slip:** it prints on the **closer's** terminal. Extra lines:
    - "Shift of: CAROL"
    - "Closed by: SUPER1 · Reason: Cashier left without closing"
    - "Counted alone", if it applies
7. **Audit and notice:**
    - The audit entry records who closed it, whose shift it was, the reason, the count and the difference.
    - The cashier sees a notice at their next login: "Your shift on Counter A was closed by SUPER1 on Sun 23:40. Difference: −100." They must tap **OK** to dismiss it.

**Pending payments:** if the shift has **pending (not completed) payments** on a part-paid ticket, the screen lists them, and the closer chooses one of:
- **Move them to my shift:** only if the closer has an open shift on this terminal.
- **Remove them:** audited, and the ticket goes back to unpaid.

A shift can't be closed while it holds pending payments. Settled payments are already in the count.

**Logging out doesn't change.** There's no prompt about an open shift when a cashier logs out (the owner's decision). The shift simply stays open until someone closes it.

## Part D — Tests

**Permission:**
- Supervisor and Admin can close another shift by default, and Cashier and Waiter can't.
- The key can't be set to Needs approval.
- Setting it to Not allowed for Supervisor hides the button and refuses the API.

**Closing:**
- From the Close day screen, My shift, and the admin site.
- From a different terminal than the shift's own.
- The blind count applies.
- A reason is required.

**Sign-off:**
- Another user's PIN works.
- Signing off alone needs the tick box, and is flagged on the slip, the report and the audit log.

**Accounting:** the over/short, the move to the safe, and the shift's lock are the same as a normal close. Every journal entry balances.

**Pending payments:** they block the close until moved or removed. Moving needs the closer's own open shift on that terminal.

**The day:** after another cashier's shift is closed, Close day succeeds (if no tickets are open).

**Notice:** the cashier's next-login notice shows, and is dismissed with OK.

**Golden slips:** the cash-up slip closed by someone else, with and without "Counted alone", at 42 and 32 columns.
